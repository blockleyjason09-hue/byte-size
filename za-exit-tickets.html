Exit ticket: straight-line graphs

  2. Does the point (3; 7) lie on the line? Show why.Yes: 2(3) + 1 = 7

Grade 11 · Probability4 min

Exit ticket: independent events

  1. A and B are independent. P(A) = 0,3 and P(B) = 0,5. Find P(A and B).0,15
  2. Are A and B mutually exclusive? Explain.No. P(A and B) ≠ 0, so they can happen together.

Rate your confidence from 1 to 5 and write one thing you're still unsure about.

Plenary ideas

Quick ways to close any lesson, in any subject.

Any subject

3-2-1

Pupils write 3 things they learned, 2 questions they still have and 1 thing they found interesting.

Any subject

Hinge question

One multiple-choice question where each wrong answer reveals a specific misconception. Everyone shows their answer at once.

Any subject

Muddiest point

Each pupil writes the one part of the lesson that is least clear. Sort the responses to plan the next lesson.

Any subject

Mistake hunt

Show a worked solution with one error. Pupils find it, explain it and correct it.

Any subject

Explain it to a Year 7

Pupils write a two-sentence explanation of today's idea for a younger pupil.

Any subject

Traffic lights

Pupils hold up red, amber or green to show how confident they feel. Ask a green to help a red.
