The large data set

Pearson Edexcel A level Maths exams expect you to know the large data set: Met Office weather data from eight stations. Learn what is in it here, then test yourself.

At a glance

Source
The Met Office
Years
1987 and 2015
Months
May to October only (184 days a year)
Stations
5 in the UK, 3 overseas
tr
A trace of rain, less than 0.05 mm. Take it as 0.
n/a
Not available. Leave it out of calculations.

Exam tips

Context
Use the location: hemisphere, coast or inland, north or south.
Limits
Only six months and two years: be careful with claims about the whole year or long-term trends.
Check
Is the variable recorded for that station? Sunshine, cloud, humidity, gusts and visibility are UK only.
Units
Knots, oktas, decametres and hPa come up often.

North to south for the UK, then overseas.

Things worth remembering

Facts that help in “use your knowledge of the large data set” questions.

Seasons

Perth is in winter

May to October is autumn and winter in Perth, so its temperatures fall while the northern stations warm up.

Rainfall

Beijing’s wet summer

Most of Beijing’s rain falls in July and August, so its rainfall is very skewed.

Storms

Jacksonville

Hot and humid, with occasional very high rainfall and wind from tropical storms later in the period.

Wind

Coast versus inland

Coastal stations such as Camborne and Leuchars tend to be windier than inland Heathrow and Leeming.

Fog

Humidity and visibility

Humidity near 100% with very low visibility suggests mist or fog.

Cleaning

Missing values

Some days have n/a. Remove them and adjust n before finding a mean or proportion.

Quick check

Choose an answer, then open the worked solution.

Recall

Which months does the large data set cover?

Worked solution
Every station has data from 1 May to 31 October: 184 days in each of 1987 and 2015. Answer C.
Recall

Which variable is recorded for Perth?

Worked solution
The overseas stations only have temperature, rainfall, pressure and windspeed. Answer D.
Beaufort

The daily mean windspeed at Leuchars is 14 knots. Which Beaufort description is given?

Worked solution
11 to 16 knots is Moderate (Beaufort force 4). Answer B.
Cleaning data

A rainfall column reads 2.4, tr, 0, n/a, 5.1. What is the mean daily rainfall?

Worked solution
tr counts as 0 and n/a is left out: (2.4 + 0 + 0 + 5.1) ÷ 4 = 1.875 mm. Answer B.
Context

Why are Perth’s July temperatures lower than Jacksonville’s?

Worked solution
The seasons are reversed in the southern hemisphere. Answer B.
Units

A visibility of 350 dam is how far?

Worked solution
1 decametre = 10 m, so 350 dam = 3500 m = 3.5 km. Answer C.
