TMUA & ESAT

Admissions tests for maths, computer science, economics, engineering and the sciences. Here is what each test looks like, with practice questions to try.

TMUA

Test of Mathematics for University Admission

Papers
Paper 1: Applications of mathematical knowledge
Paper 2: Mathematical reasoning
Questions
20 per paper, 5 options each
Time
75 minutes per paper
Calculator
Not allowed
Score
1.0 to 9.0
Format
On screen at a test centre

ESAT

Engineering and Science Admissions Test

Modules
Mathematics 1 (everyone sits this), plus modules chosen by the course: Mathematics 2, Physics, Chemistry or Biology
Maths modules
27 questions in 40 minutes each
Calculator
Not allowed
Score
1.0 to 9.0 per module
Marking
No marks lost for wrong answers

Always check dates, deadlines and requirements on the official ESAT and TMUA website and on each university's course page.

Practice questions

Tap an answer to check it, then open the worked solution.

TMUA Paper 1 style

What is the sum of the solutions of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0?

Worked solution
Factorise: (2x − 1)(x − 3) = 0, so x = 1/2 or 3. The sum is 7/2. Faster: the sum of the roots of ax² + bx + c is −b/a = 7/2. Answer B.
TMUA Paper 2 style

Which statement is the contrapositive of: “If n² is even, then n is even”?

Worked solution
The contrapositive of “if P then Q” is “if not Q then not P”. Here that is “if n is odd, then n² is odd”. Option A is the converse and C is the inverse. Answer B.
TMUA Paper 2 style

The statement “for all real x, x² > x” is false. Which value of x is a counterexample?

Worked solution
When x = 1/2, x² = 1/4, which is less than 1/2. Every other option satisfies x² > x. Answer C.
TMUA Paper 1 style

Evaluate ∫02 (3x² − 2x) dx.

Worked solution
[x³ − x²] from 0 to 2 gives (8 − 4) − 0 = 4. Answer B.
ESAT Mathematics 1 style

A rectangle's length is increased by 20% and its width is decreased by 20%. What happens to its area?

Worked solution
The new area is 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 of the original, which is a 4% decrease. Answer B.
ESAT Mathematics 2 style

How many real solutions does x⁴ − 5x² + 4 = 0 have?

Worked solution
Treat it as a quadratic in x²: (x² − 1)(x² − 4) = 0, so x = ±1 or ±2. That gives four real solutions. Answer E.

How to prepare

  1. Learn the specification first. Both tests only use content from a published list, mostly GCSE and AS level maths.
  2. Practise without a calculator. Fast, accurate mental arithmetic and fraction work saves minutes.
  3. Work to time. TMUA gives under 4 minutes per question and ESAT under 1½. Leave hard questions and come back.
  4. Always answer. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so never leave a blank.
  5. Study logic for TMUA Paper 2. Converse, contrapositive, counterexamples and “necessary and sufficient” come up again and again.
